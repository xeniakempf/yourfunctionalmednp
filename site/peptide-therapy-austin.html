What are peptides?

Your body's own messengers

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules. They help cells communicate and switch on processes like healing, growth, hormone regulation and repair. Different peptides target different functions, so your protocol is matched to your goals and labs.

What they can support

  • Repair and recovery
  • Weight and fat loss
  • Muscle and strength
  • Sleep

 

  • Gut health
  • Focus and mood
  • Immune function
  • Healthy aging

Not a quick fix. Peptides are a complementary part of your functional medicine plan, prescribed for your individual needs and never as a standalone treatment.

Questions

Peptide FAQ

More answers about the practice are in the general FAQ.

What are peptides and how do they work?

Short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules. They help cells communicate and stimulate processes such as healing, growth and regulation.

Are peptides safe?

Peptides are safest when prescribed and monitored by a licensed provider. That's why every protocol here starts with labs and stays under clinician supervision.

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. A consultation reviews your goals and health status before you start. Refills of the same peptides don't need another consult.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on the peptide and your goals. Some people notice changes within weeks; others see the biggest benefits after 2–3 months of consistent use.

How are peptides taken?

Most are small injections under the skin that you give at home. A few, such as BPC-157 and 5-Amino 1MQ, also come as oral options, and GHK-Cu is available topically.

Are peptides a replacement for other treatment?

No. Peptides complement your functional medicine plan, including nutrition, lifestyle and any other treatment you need. They aren't prescribed as a standalone fix.
