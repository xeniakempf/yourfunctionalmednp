The Benefits of Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT) for Women

By Xenia Kempf, FNP-C · August 30, 2025 · 3 min read

Menopause is a natural phase of life, but that doesn’t mean you need to struggle with the changes it brings. Hot flashes, night sweats, fatigue, mood swings, brain fog, and weight gain are all common symptoms that can affect your quality of life. Beyond symptoms, low hormone levels also impact your bone health, heart health, brain function, and metabolism.

This is where Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT) can make a life-changing difference.

What is BHRT?

BHRT uses hormones that are structurally identical to the ones your body naturally makes. Because of this, your body recognizes and processes them the same way it would your own hormones. This differs from some traditional hormone replacement therapies (HRT), which may use synthetic or animal-derived hormones that don’t perfectly match human hormones.

Which Hormones Are Used in BHRT for Women?

BHRT is personalized, but most women benefit from a combination of the following:

  • Estradiol (Estrogen): Supports bone health, brain function, cardiovascular protection, skin, mood, and sexual health.
  • Progesterone: Promotes calm, restorative sleep, balances estrogen, protects the uterine lining, and improves mood.
  • Testosterone (in small doses for women): Supports libido, energy, muscle strength, and mental clarity.
  • DHEA: A precursor hormone that can help with energy, mood, and resilience.

Benefits of BHRT for Women

  • Relief from hot flashes and night sweats
  • Improved sleep quality
  • More stable mood and reduced anxiety/irritability
  • Increased energy and mental clarity
  • Improved sexual health and libido
  • Protection for bones (lower osteoporosis risk)
  • Positive impact on heart and blood vessels

In short, BHRT doesn’t just treat symptoms — it helps protect long-term health.

What About Safety and Cancer Risk?

One of the most common concerns women have is whether hormone therapy increases the risk of cancer, especially breast cancer.

Here’s what research shows:

  • Large, modern studies indicate that bioidentical hormones — particularly transdermal estradiol (patch/cream) combined with oral micronized progesterone — have a very favorable safety profile.
  • The risk of breast cancer with this type of BHRT appears no higher than in women not taking hormones, and in some studies, it may even be lower than with synthetic hormones used in older HRT approaches.
  • Progesterone (the bioidentical form) is protective of the uterus and works differently from synthetic progestins, which were linked to higher risks in the Women’s Health Initiative (WHI) study.

In other words: BHRT, when properly prescribed and monitored, is considered safe for most women.

The Bottom Line

Menopause can feel overwhelming, but you don’t have to “just live with it.” BHRT offers a safe, effective, and natural way to restore balance, protect long-term health, and help you feel like yourself again.

If you’ve been worried about hormones and cancer, know this: when prescribed thoughtfully and monitored carefully, bioidentical hormones are one of the safest and most effective tools we have for supporting women through midlife and beyond.
