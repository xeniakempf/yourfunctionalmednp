Is it right for you?

More than a number on the scale

GLP-1 therapy helps control appetite, steady blood sugar and support lasting fat loss without fad diets. Your eligibility is confirmed after a review of your health history and goals.

Who it's for

  • BMI of 30 or higher
  • BMI of 27 or higher with a weight-related condition such as diabetes or high blood pressure
  • BMI over 24 with blood sugar, inflammation or other metabolic goals
  • Patients with insulin resistance, metabolic syndrome or weight-loss resistance, independent of BMI

Especially helpful for

  • Prediabetes
  • PCOS
  • Weight gain in perimenopause and menopause
  • Food cravings and constant hunger
  • Fatty liver

How GLP-1s work

Fewer cravings, steadier blood sugar

GLP-1 medications mimic a hormone your gut already makes. You feel full sooner and stay full longer, and your blood sugar becomes easier to control. Tirzepatide also acts on the GIP receptor for added effects on insulin sensitivity and appetite.

In the body

  • Release insulin when blood sugar is high
  • Reduce appetite and cravings
  • Slow stomach emptying
  • Lower glucagon release

Beyond weight loss

  • Better insulin sensitivity and lower HbA1c
  • Lower blood pressure and cholesterol
  • Less inflammation
  • Improved fatty liver markers in some patients

A whole-body approach

Medication works best with support

I aim to keep doses low for healthy, sustainable weight loss, and pair medication with the habits that protect muscle and keep weight off once you reach your goal.

Nutrition and movement

  • Protein, healthy fats and fiber to steady blood sugar
  • Strength training to protect muscle and bone

Rest and resilience

  • Sleep that supports insulin sensitivity
  • Stress tools: breathwork, yoga, mindfulness
  • Emotional support and accountability

Questions

GLP-1 FAQ

More answers about the practice are in the general FAQ.

What are GLP-1 medications?

Prescription injections that support weight loss by regulating appetite, slowing stomach emptying and improving blood sugar control. Semaglutide acts on the GLP-1 receptor; tirzepatide acts on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors.

Are they FDA-approved for weight loss?

Brand-name semaglutide (Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Zepbound) are FDA-approved for chronic weight management. Compounded versions are prepared by licensed pharmacies and are not FDA-approved. We'll talk through which option fits you.

How much weight can I lose?

In clinical studies, people lost an average of 12–20% of their body weight over time, alongside nutrition and lifestyle changes. Results vary from person to person.

How are they taken?

As a once-weekly injection under the skin. I'll show you how to do it at home.

What are the side effects?

The most common are nausea, vomiting, constipation and reduced appetite. Starting at a low dose and increasing gradually helps keep them manageable.

How long will I need treatment?

Many patients continue long-term, while others taper once they reach their goal. Your plan is reassessed regularly based on your progress and how you feel.

Do I need to come in person?

No. All GLP-1 consults and follow-ups are virtual, available anywhere in Texas.
