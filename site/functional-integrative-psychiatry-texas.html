Advanced testing

It starts with data

Metabolic psychiatry looks at how your brain and body systems interact. Testing shows what is contributing to your symptoms, so treatment is precise rather than trial and error.

What we look at

  • Vitamins, minerals and amino acids
  • Gut health and inflammation
  • Cortisol, thyroid and hormones
  • Heavy metals and toxins
  • Genetics and medication response
  • Stress, sleep and lifestyle

Why it matters

  • Finds deficiencies that affect mood, focus and energy
  • Shows inflammation linked to anxiety and depression
  • Predicts medication sensitivities
  • Guides a plan built for your biochemistry

Questions

Integrative psychiatry FAQ

More answers about the practice are in the general FAQ.

How is metabolic psychiatry different from traditional psychiatry?

It looks beyond symptoms to what is causing anxiety, depression, fatigue or mood changes. Along with medication when it's needed, it uses lab testing, nutrition, hormones, gut health and lifestyle to restore balance.

Who can benefit?

Anyone with anxiety, depression, burnout, fatigue, brain fog, insomnia or emotional ups and downs, especially people who haven't found relief with medication alone or want a root-cause approach.

What tests might you order?

Depending on your symptoms: hormones, thyroid, cortisol, gut microbiome, inflammation markers, genetics and nutrient levels. The results guide a more precise plan.

Can you manage or taper my medications?

Yes. Integrative medication management means we can continue, adjust or taper medications safely while treating the underlying causes with nutrition, supplements and lifestyle support.

Do you prescribe stimulants for ADHD?

No. I treat ADHD with non-stimulant options, along with nutrition, testing and lifestyle support.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Most patients notice changes in mood, energy and focus within a few weeks. Deeper metabolic and hormonal balance can take several months.

Are visits in person or virtual?

Both. In-person visits are at 6850 Austin Center Blvd, Building Two, Suite 320 (inside Therapy Space), and video visits are available anywhere in Texas.
