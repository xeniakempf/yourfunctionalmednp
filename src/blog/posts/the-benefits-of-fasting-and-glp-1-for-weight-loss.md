## Types of Fasting

There are several types of fasting, each with unique benefits:

- **Intermittent Fasting (IF)** – Cycling between periods of eating and fasting (e.g., 16:8, 18:6, or 20:4 windows).

- **Time-Restricted Eating (TRE)** – Eating within a specific time frame daily, such as an 8-hour window.

- **Prolonged Fasting (24-72 Hours)** – Extended fasting periods designed to promote **deep cellular autophagy and metabolic reset** .

- **Alternate-Day Fasting** – Fasting every other day or consuming **very few calories** on fasting days.

- **Fasting-Mimicking Diet (FMD)** – A structured plan that mimics fasting benefits while allowing limited nutrient intake.
