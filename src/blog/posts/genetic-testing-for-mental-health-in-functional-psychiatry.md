## The Science Behind Genetic Testing in Psychiatry

Your genes don’t determine your destiny—but they do shape your biology. Variations in certain genes (called **SNPs** , or single nucleotide polymorphisms) can influence:

- How your body metabolizes medications

- How efficiently you produce and recycle neurotransmitters

- Your detox and methylation capacity

- Your inflammation, oxidative stress, and hormone regulation

Functional psychiatry looks at these genetic factors not in isolation, but as part of your whole biochemical picture.

## How Genetic Testing Guides Functional Psychiatry

Genetic testing can reveal subtle biological vulnerabilities that affect mental health outcomes. Common gene categories we assess include:

### 1. Methylation & Detoxification (MTHFR, COMT, GST, SOD2)

- **MTHFR** affects folate metabolism, which impacts serotonin and dopamine production. - *Variant impact:* Low motivation, anxiety, treatment-resistant depression, fatigue.

- **COMT** influences how quickly you break down dopamine and norepinephrine. - *Variant impact:* Fast COMT = low dopamine (focus issues); slow COMT = high dopamine (anxiety, overstimulation).

- **GST & SOD2** relate to antioxidant and detox capacity—key for clearing neurotoxins and reducing inflammation.

### 2. Neurotransmitter Metabolism (MAO-A, GAD1, TPH2)

- **MAO-A** regulates serotonin and dopamine breakdown; slow variants may increase irritability or anxiety.

- **GAD1** is involved in converting glutamate to calming GABA; low activity may cause excitability or insomnia.

- **TPH2** helps produce serotonin from tryptophan; variants can lower serotonin synthesis.

### 3. Inflammation & Brain Resilience (IL6, TNF, BDNF)

- **IL6 and TNF** variants can increase inflammation, raising risk for depression or fatigue.

- **BDNF** supports neuroplasticity; lower activity is linked to poor stress adaptation and slower recovery from trauma.

### 4. Hormone & Stress Regulation (NR3C1, CRHR1)

- These genes affect cortisol and adrenal signaling. Imbalances can contribute to anxiety, burnout, or difficulty adapting to stress.

## Why Genetic Insight Matters for Mental Health

Understanding your genetic makeup helps us:

- **Predict medication sensitivity** (e.g., SSRI side effects or poor responders)

- **Personalize supplements**

- **Optimize detoxification and antioxidant support**

- **Design nutrition and lifestyle interventions** that match your biochemical blueprint

This precision approach reduces side effects, improves treatment response, and prevents relapse by addressing your unique vulnerabilities.

## Beyond the Genome: Epigenetics & Lifestyle

Genes load the gun—but environment and individual actions pull the trigger.Through nutrition, sleep, stress management, and toxin reduction, we can **turn genes on or off** in favorable ways. This concept—called **epigenetics** —empowers patients to take control of their mental health outcomes.

## Is Genetic Testing Right for You?

You may benefit from genetic testing if you have:

- A history of poor medication response or side effects

- Anxiety, depression, or fatigue that persists despite treatment

- Family history of mood disorders, ADHD, or addiction

- Suspected methylation or detox issues

- A desire for a data-driven, root-cause approach
