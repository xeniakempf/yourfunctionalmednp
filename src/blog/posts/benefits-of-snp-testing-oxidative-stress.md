### What Is Oxidative Stress and Why Does It Matter?

![A close up of a blue and purple DNA structure](local:inline/benefits-of-snp-testing-oxidative-stress-0.webp) {cap: DNA Double Helix}

Oxidative stress is a biological imbalance that occurs when the body produces more **reactive oxygen species (ROS)** and **reactive nitrogen species (RNS)** than it can neutralize. These highly reactive molecules can cause damage to **lipids, DNA, RNA, and proteins** , leading to cellular dysfunction, premature aging, and increased risk for chronic diseases.

While some level of oxidative stress is necessary for immune function and cell signaling, chronic oxidative stress has been linked to a wide range of health concerns, including:

- **Cardiovascular disease**

- **Neurodegenerative disorders** (Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s)

- **Cancer development**

- **Autoimmune conditions**

- **Metabolic disorders** (diabetes, obesity)

- **Chronic inflammation** and accelerated aging

Because oxidative stress can have a **systemic impact on health** , understanding how your body naturally defends itself against these free radicals is crucial. ### Key Antioxidant Genes That Influence Oxidative Stress

Genetic SNP testing for oxidative stress typically focuses on genes that influence **antioxidant enzyme activity** and **free radical neutralization** . Some key genes tested include:

- **SOD 1-3 (Superoxide Dismutase 1-3):** They encode an essential enzyme that neutralizes superoxide radicals in mitochondria. Variations can lead to increased oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction.

- **GPX1 (Glutathione Peroxidase 1):** A critical antioxidant enzyme that helps break down hydrogen peroxide into water, protecting cells from oxidative damage.

- **CAT (Catalase):** Converts hydrogen peroxide into oxygen and water, preventing cellular damage.

- **NQO1 (NAD(P)H Quinone Dehydrogenase 1):** Plays a key role in detoxifying oxidative species and supporting cellular antioxidant defense.

- **GSTP1 (Glutathione S-Transferase Pi 1):** Affects glutathione metabolism and detoxification. SNPs in this gene can impact the body’s ability to clear toxins and neutralize oxidative damage.

- **NOS3 (Endothelial Nitric Oxide Synthase):** Helps regulate nitric oxide production, which is crucial for cardiovascular health and inflammation control.

By identifying genetic variations in these antioxidant-related genes, SNP testing can **highlight potential vulnerabilities** in an individual’s ability to handle oxidative stress.
