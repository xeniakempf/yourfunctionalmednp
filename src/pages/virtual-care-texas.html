Why virtual care

Waiting weeks for a doctor shouldn't be normal

Most of Texas doesn't have enough primary care. 224 of the state's 254 counties are federally designated shortage areas for health professionals. That can mean weeks of waiting for a new-patient appointment, long drives to the nearest clinic, and rushed visits once you get there. Virtual care closes that distance.

Questions

Virtual care FAQ

More answers about the practice are in the general FAQ.

Do I need to live near Austin?

No. Every service is available by secure video anywhere in Texas, including Midland, Odessa, Beaumont, Lubbock, Amarillo, San Angelo, El Paso, Tyler, Waco, Corpus Christi, the Rio Grande Valley, Houston, Dallas and San Antonio. In-person visits are available in Austin if you prefer.

Do I need to be in Texas during my visit?

Yes. You need to be in Texas at the time of each video appointment.

Where do I get my labs drawn?

At a lab near you. Bloodwork can be drawn locally, many functional tests are done with at-home kits, and imaging such as X-ray, ultrasound, mammogram, CT and MRI is ordered to a facility in your area.

Can prescriptions go to my local pharmacy?

Yes. Prescriptions can go to your local pharmacy, a mail-order pharmacy or a cash-pay compounding pharmacy, and I'll help you find the most affordable option. I don't prescribe controlled substances such as opioids, sedatives or stimulant ADHD medications; testosterone is the one exception.

What if I need a hands-on exam?

Most primary care concerns can be handled safely by video. If you need hands-on care, imaging or labs, I'll refer you to a local facility. In an emergency, call 911.

Can I use my insurance?

Visits are cash-pay, which allows longer visits and faster access. You can use HSA or FSA funds, and your insurance can still cover outside labs, imaging and prescriptions.

How quickly can I be seen?

Primary care members can usually be seen the same or next day. For other services, start with a free 15-minute call and we'll find the next available appointment.
