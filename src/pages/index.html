Questions

Good to know

Questions about a specific service are answered on that service's page.

Do you take insurance?

No. The practice is cash-pay, which allows longer visits and transparent pricing. You can use HSA or FSA funds, and I provide a superbill after each visit so you can request out-of-network reimbursement. Many patients file through the Reimbursify app.

Do I need to live in Texas?

Yes. I see patients in person at the Austin office, or by secure video anywhere in Texas. For video visits, you need to be in Texas at the time of the appointment.

Are labs included in the visit fee?

No. Functional medicine labs are billed separately because insurance doesn't cover them. Basic bloodwork can often be run through your insurance.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on what's going on. Most patients have about three follow-ups in the first six months after their new patient visit, and see improvement within the first eight weeks.

Can you be my primary care provider?

Yes, through the Integrative Primary Care membership: primary and urgent care visits, messaging, prescriptions, and labs twice a year, for adults anywhere in Texas.

Can you prescribe medications?

Yes, including hormone therapy, GLP-1s, mental health medications and antibiotics when appropriate. I don't prescribe controlled substances such as opioids, sedatives or stimulants; testosterone is the one exception.

How do appointments and messages work?

Everything runs through the Practice Better app: booking, video visits, lab results and secure messages. Questions about your plan between visits are answered through the portal at no extra charge.
