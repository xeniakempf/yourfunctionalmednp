How Functional Medicine Can Decrease ADHD Symptoms

By Xenia Kempf, FNP-C · February 13, 2025 · 3 min read

Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) is often thought of as a childhood condition, but many adults continue to struggle with symptoms like inattention, impulsivity, and restlessness. While conventional treatments often focus on medication, functional medicine takes a root-cause approach, addressing the underlying imbalances that contribute to ADHD symptoms. If you’ve been searching for a functional & integrative medicine provider in Austin, look no further! I can help you decrease your symptoms with simple interventions. You may be surprised at how much lifestyle, diet, and metabolic health can influence your focus and mental clarity. Read below how functional medicine can decrease ADHD symptoms without the use of stimulants.

Understanding the Root Causes of ADHD Symptoms

ADHD symptoms can be influenced by a variety of biological and environmental factors, including:

1. Nutrient Deficiencies Low levels of omega-3 fatty acids, zinc, magnesium, and B vitamins can impact neurotransmitter production, leading to difficulties with focus, impulse control, and emotional regulation.

2. Gut Health and the Gut-Brain Connection The gut produces neurotransmitters like dopamine and serotonin, which are essential for cognitive function and emotional balance. Conditions like leaky gut, dysbiosis, and food sensitivities (such as gluten and dairy intolerance) can contribute to brain fog, hyperactivity, and inattention.

3. Blood Sugar Imbalances Fluctuations in blood sugar levels can lead to mood swings, poor concentration, and difficulty staying on task. A diet high in processed foods and refined sugars can worsen ADHD symptoms by causing spikes and crashes in energy levels.

4. Inflammation & Toxin Exposure Chronic inflammation, environmental toxins (such as heavy metals and pesticides), and high oxidative stress can contribute to neurological dysfunction, affecting memory, focus, and executive function.

5. Hormone & Neurotransmitter Imbalances Low levels of dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin are commonly associated with ADHD. Additionally, thyroid imbalances, adrenal dysfunction, and chronic stress can exacerbate symptoms.

How can I, a Functional & Integrative Medicine Provider in Austin help?

Unlike conventional approaches that rely solely on medication, functional medicine focuses on identifying and addressing these underlying issues. A functional medicine provider will often use comprehensive and specialty lab testing to uncover imbalances and create a customized treatment plan that may include:

✅ Nutrient Optimization – Personalized supplementation of B vitamins, omega-3s, magnesium, and zinc to support brain function.

✅ Gut Healing Protocols – Identifying and eliminating inflammatory foods, adding probiotics, and restoring gut balance.

✅ Blood Sugar Stabilization – Implementing a whole-food diet rich in healthy fats, proteins, and fiber to prevent energy crashes and improve focus.

✅ Detoxification Support – Reducing toxin exposure and supporting the body’s natural detox pathways to lower inflammation.

✅ Lifestyle & Behavioral Strategies – Incorporating mindfulness, exercise, and stress management techniques to improve attention and executive function.
