How mold affects the body

The exposure ends. The toxins stay.

Mycotoxins can stay stored in the body and keep driving inflammation long after you leave a moldy building. Over time, that ongoing load can lead to a persistent inflammatory state known as Chronic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (CIRS).

What mycotoxins disrupt

  • The gut microbiome
  • The immune system
  • Hormones and adrenal function
  • Liver and gallbladder detox

What can follow

  • Chronic inflammation (CIRS)
  • Mast cell activation and histamine reactions
  • Autoimmune activity
  • New food and chemical sensitivities

The detox protocol

Four phases, in the right order

Starting binders before the body is ready can make symptoms worse. Each phase prepares for the next, and your plan is tailored to your labs and history.

  1. Remove the source

    Find and stop ongoing exposure at home, work or school: water-damaged areas, HVAC systems, and hidden leaks in bathrooms, laundry rooms, roofs or windows.

  2. Prepare the body

    Stabilize the gut, support bile flow, and make sure the liver and kidneys can clear toxins, with hydration, electrolytes, regular bowel movements and nutrients.

  3. Bind and eliminate

    Binders matched to your mycotoxin profile, from food- and supplement-based options to prescription cholestyramine, plus glutathione and antifungals when the time is right.

  4. Repair and rebuild

    Heal the gut lining with L-glutamine, zinc carnosine and probiotics, calm the immune response, balance hormones and rebuild mitochondrial energy.

Questions

Mold detox FAQ

Not sure mold is the issue? Take the mold illness quiz.

How do I know if mold is affecting me?

Chronic fatigue, brain fog, sinus pressure, anxiety, or food and chemical sensitivities, especially after water damage or time in musty spaces, may point to mold. Testing can confirm exposure.

Can mold cause autoimmune disease or histamine problems?

Long-term exposure can overstimulate the immune system, leading to chronic inflammation, autoimmune activity, and mast cell activation with histamine-related symptoms.

What helps detox mold from the body?

Removing the exposure, supporting drainage pathways (liver, kidneys, lymph and gut) with hydration, fiber and nutrients, using binders, boosting antioxidants, repairing the gut lining, and adding prescription antifungals at the right time.

Can I do a mold detox on my own?

It isn't recommended. Starting binders or supplements before your body is ready can make symptoms worse. A provider can match the approach to your toxin load and health.

How long does mold detox take?

It depends on severity and how fully the source is removed. Some people improve within weeks; most protocols take 6–12 months.

Do you see patients outside Austin?

Yes. Visits are available by video anywhere in Texas, and lab testing can be done locally.
